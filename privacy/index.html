JobStamp for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 August 2026

JobStamp is a GPS timestamp camera and field-report app. This Privacy Policy explains what stays on your device and what is sent to service providers when you use network-backed features.

Information stored on your device

JobStamp stores photos, stamped copies, PDF reports, location evidence, and project data on your device. JobStamp has no server of its own and does not upload your photos, reports, or project content. It has no account system, advertising SDK, or general-purpose analytics SDK.

Cloud backup of JobStamp app data is disabled. Android may copy app data during a direct device-to-device migration to a new phone; that is not an upload to a JobStamp server.

Location, address, and map requests

If you grant location permission while the camera is open, JobStamp obtains a device location fix. To produce an address, it may pass the full latitude and longitude to Android's platform Geocoder. The Geocoder may use a network-backed system implementation. A three-decimal grid cell is used only to cache the result; it does not round the coordinates before the live Geocoder request.

JobStamp also prefetches map images while a location session is active. It passes the full coordinates to a map renderer centered at zoom level 17. On the OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap path, the requested style and tile addresses reveal the small map area around that location. On a build configured with a Google Maps key and a device with Google Play Services, JobStamp first uses the Google Maps SDK for Android. If that Google render fails, JobStamp may request the same area from OpenFreeMap as a fallback, so both providers can be contacted during one render attempt.

The map-stamp setting controls whether a map image is used in the stamp. In the current version it does not stop background map prefetch while the camera has an active location session. Separately, opening a photo or project map requests OpenFreeMap tiles for the stored photo/project area and lets you pan and zoom. You can prevent new camera-location, reverse-geocode, and prefetch requests by denying or revoking location permission. Avoid opening map screens if you do not want stored location areas sent to the tile service.

Map and Geocoder providers receive network information such as an IP address. The Google Maps SDK also automatically collects request and device metadata, SDK/build information, stack traces and crash metrics from SDK code, an Internal Usage Attribution Identifier, and a Maps SDK-specific pseudonymous identifier. Because JobStamp invokes the Map Camera APIs, Google may also collect map camera events. Google states that it uses these data to maintain and improve Google services.

Google's current Maps Platform terms state that Google receives and may retain IP addresses and latitude/longitude coordinates and that Google and the app publisher act under controller-to-controller data protection terms. Use of Google Maps features is also subject to the Google Maps End User Additional Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

OpenFreeMap states that its normal server logs exclude IP addresses, but that it may temporarily log IP addresses during a security incident for up to 30 days; anonymized server logs may be retained indefinitely. See the OpenFreeMap Privacy Policy.

Billing and subscriptions

If you buy or restore a subscription, Google Play processes the payment. JobStamp uses RevenueCat to validate subscription status. RevenueCat receives the Google Play purchase token and an anonymous app user ID and retains purchase information needed to validate and restore entitlement status. JobStamp does not give RevenueCat a JobStamp account name, email address, or photo/project content.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Crash and diagnostic information

JobStamp does not include a dedicated crash-reporting SDK. However, when the Google Maps SDK is invoked, that SDK may collect stack traces for crashes in SDK code, crash metrics, diagnostics, request/device metadata, and its pseudonymous SDK identifier as described above. Separately, standard Google Play system reporting (Play Vitals) may provide Google with crash or ANR statistics from devices whose users enabled Android usage and diagnostics sharing.

For more information, see the Maps SDK data disclosure and Google Play crash and ANR help.

Retention and deletion

On the free tier, JobStamp keeps photo originals on the device for at least 7 days after capture and removes an original only after its stamped copy has been saved to the device gallery. Pro keeps originals on the device. You can delete local data with JobStamp's deletion controls or remove the app and its private data by uninstalling it.

For a request concerning billing data held through RevenueCat or data that we can reasonably link to JobStamp's provider records, contact jobstampapp@gmail.com. We will process a verifiable request where identification is possible. Google and OpenFreeMap also apply their own retention and deletion rules; their policies linked above explain the rights and controls they offer.

Children

JobStamp is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when JobStamp's data practices change. We will post the updated policy at https://jobstamp.github.io/privacy/ and update the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact jobstampapp@gmail.com.
