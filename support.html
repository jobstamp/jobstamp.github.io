ProofPDF for iPhone · ProofPDF for Android

Support

For help with either app, email jobstampapp@gmail.com.

Restore your Pro purchase

  • iPhone: In ProofPDF, open the Pro subscription screen and tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account you used to subscribe.
  • Android: In ProofPDF, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases in the Pro section, or use the same option on the subscription screen. Use the Google Play account you used to subscribe.

Cancel a subscription

  • iPhone: Open Settings ▸ Apple ID (your name, now called your Apple Account) ▸ Subscriptions. Select ProofPDF and tap Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Open Google Play Store ▸ profile icon ▸ Payments & subscriptions ▸ Subscriptions. Select ProofPDF and tap Cancel subscription.

Deleting either app does not cancel a subscription.

Delete all data stored inside the app

On iPhone, choose Delete App for ProofPDF. On Android, uninstall ProofPDF. This removes the app and its private local data. Files you saved to Photos, Files, or another destination, and ProofPDF copies already in Google Drive, need to be removed from those locations separately.

For privacy questions or requests about provider records, use the email above.
