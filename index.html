JobStamp

A GPS timestamp camera and one-tap PDF field report, running entirely on your phone.

What it does

  • Stamps every photo with date, time, GPS coordinates, address, and an optional map.
  • Keeps the original photo untouched and writes the stamp at full resolution.
  • Turns a day of site photos into a PDF report with one tap.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics SDK, and no JobStamp server.

Support

Questions, bug reports, and privacy or deletion requests: jobstampapp@gmail.com. We read every message and normally reply within a few business days.
